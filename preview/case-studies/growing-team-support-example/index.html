Support for a growing team.

Giving employees a clear route to help with devices, software and connections.

Illustrative example. This is a proposed scenario, not a completed client project or a claim of results.

The situation

A business has added employees across its office and remote team. Staff are asking different colleagues for IT help, and recurring laptop and software issues are taking time away from their work.

A proposed approach

Review the devices and applications in use, then agree which issues the support arrangement will cover. Establish how employees request help and when an issue needs an on-site visit. Recurring problems can inform the next maintenance discussion.

Work to consider

  • Review users, devices and recurring issues
  • Agree remote and on-site support arrangements
  • Assist with workstation and software troubleshooting
  • Provide practical guidance for affected employees

What the handover would check

A real engagement could review the types of requests received and which problems continue to recur. Response expectations and coverage would be agreed before support starts. This example assumes no response-time or productivity results.
