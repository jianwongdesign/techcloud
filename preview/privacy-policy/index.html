Privacy Policy

How information is handled in this website preview.

Draft for review. TechCloud must confirm this policy before the full website launches.

The shared preview

This is a design preview. Its contact form validates entries in your browser and shows a demonstration response; it does not send or save an enquiry. Please do not enter sensitive or confidential information into a preview form.

Contacting TechCloud

Email and telephone links open your chosen application. If you contact TechCloud through those channels, you provide the information in your message directly to the business. Include only the details needed to discuss your enquiry.

Website hosting and embedded maps

The preview is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider and external services may process technical information such as your IP address and browser details when you visit. The footer contains an embedded Google Map, which connects to Google when it loads. Third-party privacy policies apply to those services.

Before the full website launches

The production privacy notice must confirm the business contact for privacy enquiries, information collected through the live form, purposes of use, service providers, retention arrangements and handling of requests. This draft does not establish those unconfirmed operational details.

Privacy enquiries

Please use the Contact page to ask TechCloud about personal information supplied directly to the business, including requests concerning access, correction or withdrawal of consent. Do not submit identity documents through this review preview.
