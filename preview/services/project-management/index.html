Project management

A clear plan and steady coordination for your next IT project.

Give your IT project a workable plan.

Office moves, equipment upgrades and system changes involve decisions across teams. Project management brings the scope, schedule and implementation activities together so everyone understands what needs to happen next.

Useful when a technology change involves several suppliers, competing deadlines or work that must be coordinated around business operations.

What we can help with

Scope and planning

Define the project requirements, boundaries and expected deliverables. Identify dependencies and the decisions needed before implementation can begin.

Implementation coordination

Coordinate agreed activities with the people involved. Consider access, equipment readiness and the sequence of work when planning the rollout.

Progress and risk communication

Keep stakeholders informed about progress, open decisions and issues. Discuss changes to scope or timing as they arise.

Handover and review

Review completed work against the agreed scope and identify outstanding items. Discuss any follow-up support needed after implementation.

A useful starting point

Bring these details to the first conversation:

  • The change you want to make and why
  • Target dates, budget and business constraints
  • The teams and suppliers involved
  • Known dependencies and how completion will be assessed

Discuss your requirements

Before we begin

Can you coordinate an office IT move?

An office move can be discussed as a defined project. Share the locations, target date, equipment and other suppliers involved so the scope can be assessed.

What happens after implementation?

The handover and any follow-up assistance are agreed as part of the project. Discuss documentation, outstanding items and ongoing support before work begins.
