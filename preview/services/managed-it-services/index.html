Managed IT services

Day-to-day management for the systems your business relies on.

Keep your attention on the business.

Servers, networks and workstations need attention between breakdowns. Managed IT brings their monitoring and maintenance into an agreed support plan, with technical help for the systems your team uses each day.

Useful when IT tasks are spread across several people, maintenance is slipping, or an internal team needs support with day-to-day infrastructure.

What we can help with

Infrastructure monitoring

Review the systems in scope and the issues that need attention. Monitoring arrangements depend on your equipment, access and operating requirements.

Maintenance and technical support

Plan routine checks and maintenance around your working hours. Agree how faults are reported and which systems the support arrangement covers.

Environment reviews

Look at the current setup, recurring problems and equipment needs. Use the review to prioritise maintenance and future changes.

Support planning

Define responsibilities, escalation contacts and the level of assistance needed. Coverage and response arrangements belong in the agreed service scope.

A useful starting point

Bring these details to the first conversation:

  • The number of users, locations and devices
  • Your current servers, network and business applications
  • Recurring issues and existing support arrangements
  • Working hours and systems that are critical to daily operations

Discuss your requirements

Before we begin

Can this work alongside our internal IT team?

Yes. Describe what your team manages today and where it needs assistance. Responsibilities can then be divided within the agreed scope.

What coverage and response times are included?

Coverage, response arrangements and any on-site requirements are agreed for your environment. Ask for these details in the proposal before choosing a support arrangement.
