IT installation & infrastructure

Equipment, networks, and systems set up for the way you work.

Build on the right foundations.

An office needs its equipment, network and workstations to work together. Plan the installation around the space, the people using it and the systems already in place, from a new office setup to an infrastructure refresh.

Useful when you are moving, adding workstations, replacing ageing equipment or preparing a new business location.

What we can help with

Server and workstation installation

Discuss equipment requirements and where each device will be used. Installation can then be planned around the agreed configuration and access needs.

Network equipment setup

Plan the networking equipment required for the space and users. Confirm existing connections, equipment compatibility and the areas that need connectivity.

Equipment provisioning

Prepare devices and their agreed software configuration. Confirm licences, permissions and the information needed before setup begins.

Office upgrades and handover

Coordinate changes with your office schedule. Discuss checks, user guidance and any onboarding needed after the installation.

A useful starting point

Bring these details to the first conversation:

  • Office layout, user count and planned growth
  • Existing equipment and items to be replaced
  • Network and application requirements
  • Access dates, installation windows and budget

Discuss your requirements

Before we begin

Can existing equipment be reused?

Share the models, condition and current configuration. Suitability can be reviewed against the requirements of the planned setup.

How is an installation priced?

The scope depends on the equipment, scale and complexity of the work. A discussion of your requirements comes before an itemised proposal.
